CAMPUSMIND INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY
OFFICIAL ADMISSIONS BROCHURE — ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027

1. GENERAL ADMISSIONS OVERVIEW
Welcome to CampusMind Institute of Technology (CMIT). Admissions to all undergraduate (UG), postgraduate (PG), and doctoral (Ph.D.) programs are conducted transparently based on merit, national entrance exam ranks, and academic performance.

2. UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMS OFFERED (4-Year B.Tech):
- B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) — Intake: 240 seats
- B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science (AI & DS) — Intake: 120 seats
- B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering (ECE) — Intake: 180 seats
- B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering (ME) — Intake: 120 seats
- B.Tech in Civil Engineering (CE) — Intake: 60 seats

=== PAGE 2 ===
3. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA FOR UNDERGRADUATE ADMISSIONS:
- Candidates must have passed 10+2 (Higher Secondary) or equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) as core subjects.
- A minimum aggregate of 60% marks in PCM (55% for SC/ST and PwD candidates) is mandatory.
- Candidates appearing for their final 10+2 examinations in 2026 are also eligible to apply provisionally.

4. ACCEPTED ENTRANCE EXAMINATIONS:
- National Level: JEE Main 2026 (Paper 1) score.
- State Level: State Engineering Entrance Examination (CET).
- Institutional Level: CampusMind Engineering Aptitude Test (CMEAT) conducted online for international and out-of-state candidates.

5. KEY ADMISSION DATES AND DEADLINES (2026):
- Online Application Portal Opens: February 15, 2026
- Phase 1 Application Deadline: May 15, 2026
- Phase 1 Seat Allocation & Counseling: June 1 - June 10, 2026
- Phase 2 Application Deadline: June 30, 2026

- Spot Round & Last Date for Document Verification: July 25, 2026
- Orientation Program & Commencement of Classes: August 10, 2026

6. APPLICATION PROCESS & DOCUMENTS REQUIRED:
Applications must be submitted online at https://admissions.campusmind.edu.
Required documents:
1. 10th and 12th Grade Marksheets / Passing Certificates
2. JEE Main / CET Rank Card 2026
3. Government ID Proof (Aadhaar / Passport)
4. Transfer and Migration Certificate from previous school
5. Category / Caste Certificate (if applicable)

7. POSTGRADUATE PROGRAMS (M.Tech, MBA, MCA):
- M.Tech in Computer Science: Requires B.E./B.Tech degree with valid GATE score.
- Master of Business Administration (MBA): Requires bachelor's degree with minimum 50% and valid CAT / MAT / GMAT score.
