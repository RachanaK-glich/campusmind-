- Semester 1 Installment: 50% of annual fees payable on or before July 31, 2026.
- Semester 2 Installment: 50% of annual fees payable on or before December 15, 2026.
- Late Fee Penalty: A grace period of 7 calendar days is provided. Subsequent delays incur a late charge of $20 per day up to 14 days.

4. INSTITUTIONAL SCHOLARSHIPS & FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE:

A. President's Merit Scholarship (100% Tuition Waiver):
- Eligibility: Top 1% national rank in JEE Main OR 98%+ aggregate in 10+2 examinations.
- Renewal Condition: Must maintain a minimum CGPA of 8.5 with no academic backlogs.

B. Dean's Academic Excellence Scholarship (50% Tuition Waiver):
- Eligibility: 92% to 97.9% aggregate marks in 10+2 or rank within top 5,000 in State CET.
- Renewal Condition: Must maintain a minimum CGPA of 8.0 with no backlogs.

C. Need-Based Financial Aid:
- Students with total annual household income below $25,000 are eligible for up to 75% tuition assistance upon submitting certified income tax returns and salary certificates.

D. Women in Engineering Scholarship:
- 25% annual tuition waiver for female students admitted to B.Tech Computer Science and AI programs to promote gender diversity in STEM.

5. FEE REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY:
- 100% refund (minus $100 processing fee) if admission is withdrawn 15 days before commencement of classes.
- 80% refund if withdrawn less than 15 days before classes start.
- No refund of tuition fee after 30 days of class commencement.
