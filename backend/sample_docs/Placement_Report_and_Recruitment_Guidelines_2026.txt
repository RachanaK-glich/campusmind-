- Backlog Policy: No active academic backlogs at the time of appearing for technical assessment or interviews.
- Attendance in T&P Training: 85% attendance in mandatory pre-placement training modules (data structures, algorithms, system design, mock interviews).

4. "ONE STUDENT ONE DREAM JOB" PLACEMENT POLICY:
To ensure equitable career opportunities for every student, the institution enforces the following policy:
1. Category A (Dream Offer: Salary >= $20,000 / year): A student who receives a Dream Offer is not permitted to appear in subsequent recruitment drives.
2. Category B (Standard Offer: Salary < $20,000 / year): A student with a Standard Offer is eligible to upgrade by appearing for up to two Category A Dream recruitment drives.
3. Code of Professional Ethics: Rejecting a confirmed corporate offer after formal acceptance without prior T&P Dean approval results in debarment from institutional placement support.
