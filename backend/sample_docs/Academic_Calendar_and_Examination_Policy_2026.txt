3. ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS & CONDONATION POLICY:
- Mandatory Minimum Attendance: Every student must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each registered theory lecture, tutorial, and laboratory course.
- Medical Condonation: Attendance between 65% and 74.9% may be condoned by the Dean of Academic Affairs exclusively on medical grounds or official representation in university athletics/competitions, upon submission of valid hospital discharge certificates within 3 days of returning to campus.
- Debarment Policy: Students with attendance strictly below 65% under all circumstances will be detained (debarred) from appearing in the End-Semester Examination for that course and must repeat the course during summer terms.

4. GRADING SYSTEM AND CGPA CALCULATION:
The institution follows a standard 10-Point Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) scale:
- Grade O (Outstanding, 90-100%): 10 Grade Points
- Grade A+ (Excellent, 80-89%): 9 Grade Points
- Grade A (Very Good, 70-79%): 8 Grade Points
- Grade B+ (Good, 60-69%): 7 Grade Points
- Grade B (Above Average, 55-59%): 6 Grade Points
- Grade C (Average, 50-54%): 5 Grade Points
- Grade P (Pass, 40-49%): 4 Grade Points
- Grade F (Fail, <40%): 0 Grade Points
