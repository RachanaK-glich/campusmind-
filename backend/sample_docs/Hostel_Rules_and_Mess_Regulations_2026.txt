CAMPUSMIND INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY
STUDENT RESIDENTIAL CODE & HOSTEL REGULATIONS — 2026-2027

1. GENERAL CODE OF CONDUCT
The CampusMind residential hostels provide a safe, secure, and disciplined living environment conducive to academic excellence. All resident students are required to uphold high standards of hygiene, mutual respect, and decorum.

2. CURFEW TIMINGS AND BIOMETRIC ENTRY:
- Weekday Curfew (Monday through Friday): All students must return to the hostel premises by 9:30 PM.
- Weekend Curfew (Saturday and Sunday): Curfew is extended to 10:30 PM.
- Night Attendance: Biometric fingerprint / facial verification attendance is recorded nightly between 9:30 PM and 10:15 PM in each residential wing.
- Late Entry / Night Out: Students requiring night-out passes must apply through the Student ERP portal at least 24 hours in advance, accompanied by written or verified email consent from their registered parent or guardian.

=== PAGE 2 ===
3. STRICTLY PROHIBITED ITEMS & SAFETY RULES:
To ensure fire safety and resident well-being, the following items are strictly prohibited inside hostel rooms:
1. High-wattage electrical appliances: Electric kettles, coil room heaters, immersion water rods, induction cooktops, toasters, and microwave ovens.
2. Combustible items: Candles, oil lamps, gas cylinders, incense sticks, and fireworks.
3. Controlled substances: Alcohol, narcotics, cigarettes, e-cigarettes/vapes, and prohibited pharmaceuticals.
4. Pets and domestic animals of any kind.
5. Weapons, sharp knives, or hazardous chemical substances.

Violation Penalty: Confiscation of unauthorized equipment, a monetary disciplinary fine of $150, and referral to the University Disciplinary Committee.

4. VISITOR POLICIES:
- Parents and immediate family members may meet students in the designated Hostel Reception Lobby between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM on weekdays and 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM on weekends.
- No external visitors, day scholars, or guests are permitted inside student residential rooms or dormitory corridors at any time.

5. MESS TIMINGS AND DINING SERVICES:
The central dining hall operates seven days a week with healthy, dietitian-approved menus:
- Breakfast: 7:30 AM – 9:15 AM
- Lunch: 12:30 PM – 2:15 PM
- Evening Refreshments & Tea: 5:00 PM – 6:15 PM
- Dinner: 7:30 PM – 9:30 PM

Dietary requirements: Vegetarian, Non-Vegetarian, Jain, and Gluten-sensitive options are served at dedicated service counters. Taking dining utensils, plates, or bulk food into personal rooms is strictly prohibited.

6. ZERO TOLERANCE POLICY ON RAGGING AND HARASSMENT:
CampusMind maintains an absolute ZERO TOLERANCE policy toward any form of ragging, bullying, physical harassment, or cyber-harassment.
- Any student found guilty of ragging is subject to immediate suspension, permanent expulsion from the institution, forfeiture of academic fees, and reporting to legal authorities.
- 24/7 Anti-Ragging Helpline: +1 (800) 555-SAFE / antiragging@campusmind.edu
- Chief Hostel Warden Office: Hostel Block C, Ground Floor | warden@campusmind.edu
